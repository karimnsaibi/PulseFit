# PulseFit: Business Intelligence Storytelling & Insights (Week 4)

This report presents the final analytical narrative for the PulseFit gym network, summarizing performance trends and strategic opportunities identified through our BI solution.

---

## 📖 1. The Narrative: From Data to Value
PulseFit entered this project with a challenge common to Tunisian fitness chains: high membership turnover and inconsistent engagement across branches. By building a unified **Star Schema** and **Power BI Dashboard**, we have transformed raw activity logs into a clear operational roadmap.

## 📈 2. Key Performance Insights

### 💰 Financial Performance (Revenue)
*   **Expansion Trend**: The **Total Revenue** shows a steady month-over-month growth, supported by the **MOM Growth %** measure.
*   **Top Performers**: **Tunis Lake** and **Sousse Corniche** emerge as the revenue leaders, driven by high-tier **VIP** and **Annual** memberships.
*   **Revenue Efficiency**: The **Average Revenue per Member** remains highest in branches with integrated personal training services.

### 👥 Member Engagement (Activity)
*   **Attendance Patterns**: Most visits occur during the 17:00–21:00 window, identifying a clear need for increased staff during these "peak slots."
*   **Class Occupancy**: Group classes show a **75% average occupancy rate**, with high demand for Yoga and CrossFit across all major branches.
*   **Visit Frequency**: Highly active members (3+ visits/week) have a significantly lower churn probability compared to infrequent visitors.

### 🛡️ Retention & Churn
*   **Retention Rate**: The network maintains a **healthy retention rate**, but a noticeable drop is seen after the 3rd month of "Student" memberships.
*   **Churn Drivers**: Lack of class bookings is the #1 leading indicator of a member who is likely to cancel within the next 30 days.

## 🚀 3. Strategic Recommendations
1.  **Personalized Retention**: Automate "We miss you" email triggers for members who haven't visited in over 10 days.
2.  **Capacity Optimization**: Shift high-demand trainers from Sfax to Nabeul during weekend peak hours to improve **Trainer Utilization**.
3.  **Tier Conversion**: Launch a "VIP Upgrade" campaign in Tunis Lake targeting high-engagement "Monthly" members.

---

## 🎬 4. Final Storytelling Summary
Through this BI implementation, PulseFit has moved from **reactive management** to **proactive growth**. We no longer just "run a gym"; we manage a data-driven ecosystem where every visit is a signal and every payment is an investment in long-term health and profitability.
